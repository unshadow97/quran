Terms

QuranicNextGen is free, and has to stay free.

Free, and not for sale

The app costs nothing and carries no advertising. That is not only a preference: the Quran text and all three translations in it are licensed for non-commercial use only, and the mushaf fonts from the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex may be used and given away but never sold.

Selling this app, or any part of it, or putting advertising on it, would break the terms the Quran text itself is here under.

The Quran text is not ours

The Uthmani text comes from Tanzil, the translations from their translators and publishers, the page fonts from the King Fahd Complex, and the reciter audio is streamed from EveryAyah rather than copied here.

Every source, its licence and how this project complies is listed in NOTICE.md, published alongside the app.

It does not grade your recitation

This app does not assess tajwid, makhraj or pronunciation, and it does not claim to. Where the transcription cannot hear clearly it says so rather than calling it a mistake.

Nothing here is a substitute for learning with a teacher.

Provided as it is

The app is given away with no warranty of any kind. It is built to keep your recordings safe — every one is written as it is made, so a crash cannot take it — but a browser can still evict storage, a device can still be lost, and no software is exempt from that.

Keep a backup. Settings has one you can download in a single tap.

What you record is yours

Your recitations, notes and progress belong to you. This project never receives them, claims no licence over them, and has no way to reach them.

The code

The code this project wrote is MIT-licensed and open. The Quran text, translations and fonts are not ours to relicense and keep their own terms, which are stricter.
